Full write-up

Qdrant is the default-quality and single-thread latency engine. It is not the throughput engine. Redis, Weaviate, and Elasticsearch usually win saturated RPS once any HNSW cell that clears a recall floor is allowed. pgvector is only the first cheap cell on glove and gist. FineWeb is not in this ranking.

Strengths

  • Quality at a cheap index. Glove’s first cell is already 0.845. Weaviate, Redis, and Elasticsearch sit at ~0.66–0.67 and need search ef=500–1000 to catch up. Elasticsearch never catches Qdrant’s gist 0.893 even at ef=1000.
  • Single-thread latency. p95 is ~3 ms on every core dataset. Redis is the only peer (faster on dbpedia: 2.50 ms). Milvus and OpenSearch are much slower under the same cell.
  • Recall ceiling. Best glove cell at search ef=100 is 0.957 (m=64, ef_construct=1000). Raising search ef on the cheap glove index reaches 0.981; nobody else breaks 0.93 on that index.
  • Stable under load. Saturated p95 stays usable. OpenSearch and Milvus cliff (OpenSearch dbpedia 220 ms; Milvus cohere 345 ms at p=100).
  • Index time is fine. Glove 42 s, with Redis / Milvus / OpenSearch. Elasticsearch is the outlier (glove 304 s, dbpedia 1,181 s).

Weaknesses

  • Saturated RPS is the consistent loss. Cheap-cell Redis is 2.1–4.5× Qdrant at lower recall. At a 0.80–0.90 floor Qdrant is typically 3rd–5th. On dbpedia / cohere at 0.98–0.99, Redis / Weaviate / Elasticsearch still deliver 2–4× Qdrant’s RPS.
  • No sloppy-fast config. At a 0.70 glove floor Qdrant is 6th of 7 on RPS because its cheapest cell is already 0.845.
  • Milvus wins raw recall on high-dim (gist 0.975, dbpedia 0.997, cohere 0.994) and pays for it with collapsed RPS (800 / 460 on gist / cohere).
  • Not the fastest to index. Redis and Weaviate finish sooner on gist / dbpedia / cohere (Redis cohere 52 s vs 131 s).

How to say it

Qdrant if you want high recall from a cheap HNSW graph and low single-thread latency. Redis or Weaviate if you want max RPS and will take a lower default recall or a higher search ef. Milvus if you want max recall on 768–1536-d and can live with the load latency. Elasticsearch is competitive on high-floor RPS and weak on index time and cheap-cell recall. OpenSearch tracks Qdrant’s recall more closely than Weaviate does, but is the slow engine under load.

The slider above is the demo: at 0.80 Qdrant looks mid-pack on RPS; at 0.98 on glove it is in the top two. These cells are a matched HNSW grid, not a max-RPS Qdrant tune — methodology note.
